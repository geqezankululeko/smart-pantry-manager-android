# Real Screenshot Checklist

The assignment requires actual screenshots of the running app. Do not replace these with mockups or design previews. Capture the following after the project runs on an emulator or physical Android device.

1. Empty Pantry screen with the friendly empty-state message.
2. Add Ingredient form before entry.
3. Validation error for a blank name or zero quantity.
4. Populated Pantry after creating an item.
5. Edit Ingredient form prefilled with an existing record.
6. Pantry after the update, with the changed quantity visible.
7. Delete confirmation dialog.
8. Pantry after confirming deletion.
9. Suggested Recipes with the zero-match message.
10. Pantry containing every ingredient needed for one seeded recipe.
11. Suggested Recipes showing that recipe after the final sufficient ingredient is added.
12. Recipe Detail showing the full requirements and method.
13. Suggested Recipes after reducing/removing one required ingredient, proving the recipe disappears.
14. Settings with expiry warnings and the default-unit selector.
15. Pantry after closing and reopening the app, proving persistence.

For each image:

- Keep the full app window visible.
- Avoid showing personal notifications, account names or unrelated desktop content.
- Use a readable emulator zoom or a clear phone screenshot.
- Give the report figure a numbered caption that states what the image proves.
